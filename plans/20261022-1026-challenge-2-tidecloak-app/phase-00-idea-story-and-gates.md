## Product scope and truthful claims

Each circle cycle is intended to have its own encrypted release package. Intended trust split: the host/operator cannot decrypt; stewards approve governed access but do not read; the winner reads only after access is granted. A quorum would authorize access to a release package, not validate transaction contents or move real money. Contributions and escrow are synthetic.

The requested B0 proof remains a live sequence: ciphertext-only host view; ORK refusal for an unauthorized steward and unreleased winner; observed quorum change request; winner decryption; mock escrow redemption. None of that live sequence is verified. The ordinary-login comparison, cycle rotation and “two of three” language must remain conditional until the relevant live behavior is observed.

Known limitations to retain in any final narrative: authentic-client assumption, possible steward collusion, master-realm-admin recovery path, and mock money/escrow. Never claim financial custody or transfer.
